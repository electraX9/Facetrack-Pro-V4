FaceTrack Pro v4.0 — Fully Offline

All AI runs locally on your device. No internet needed after setup. Powered by Google MediaPipe (face + hand) and face-api.js (emotion, age, gender).

System-wide gaze scroll works in every app on your PC — browsers, Word, Excel, PDF readers, everything. Move your cursor over the target app and look up or down.
🎯
Gaze Calibration
9-point dot-following calibration builds a personal iris→gaze map for accurate, individual scroll thresholds.
🌐
System-Wide Scroll
Gaze scroll fires Windows mouse wheel events at the cursor position — affects any app under the cursor.
🧠
Full Biometric Mesh
478-point face mesh · 2,556 triangles · iris rings + crosshairs · all contour groups with glow.
😊
Emotion AI
7-class emotion recognition with animated confidence bars, smoothed over frames.
✋
Hand Tracking
21-landmark hand model with coloured finger bones, gesture recognition, and finger drawing.
📏
Distance
Dual-method: face width + inter-pupillary distance averaged. Calibrate with C at 60 cm.

Gaze Scroll — How to Use

Calibrate first with the 🎯 Calibrate button for best accuracy. Without calibration, default thresholds are used.

Step by step

  1. Click 🎯 Calibrate and follow the red dot through all 9 positions
  2. Make sure Gaze Scroll pill is green (on)
  3. Click ⬆ Always Top so FaceTrack floats above other apps
  4. Move your mouse cursor over the window you want to scroll (browser, Word, etc.)
  5. Look up and hold for ~0.3 s → the target window scrolls up
  6. Look down and hold for ~0.3 s → scrolls down
  7. Look centre → stops immediately

Why calibration matters

Everyone's eyes sit differently. Calibration measures your personal iris ratio at known screen positions (top, middle, bottom) and computes exact thresholds for UP and DOWN. Without it, the system uses conservative defaults that may feel less responsive.

Fine-tuning

If scroll fires too easily — press U to tighten the up-zone or J to tighten the down-zone. If it's not sensitive enough, press I / K to widen zones.

Drawing Controls

Enable drawing with D (or the Draw pill), then use hand Pinch gesture or press X to clear.
DToggle finger drawing on/off
XClear entire drawing canvas
BCycle brush colour (green → cyan → pink → amber → yellow)
1Brush size: tiny (3px)
2Brush size: small (6px)
3Brush size: medium (9px)
4Brush size: large (12px)
5Brush size: extra large (15px)

To draw: enable T (hand tracking) + D (draw mode), then bring your hand up and pinch your index finger and thumb together. Move your hand to draw a stroke. Release to lift the pen.

There is no undo. Press X to clear everything and start fresh.
